Why these 100 scenarios
BeeScene selected and ranked 100 candidates using research judgment about user benefits, vehicle suitability, AI gains and validation value. No real user scores, market forecasts or weighted scores are used. Rankings do not establish value or rollout order.
Three dimensions, combined filters
Multiple selections within one dimension use OR; different dimensions use AND. Each scenario has one primary need and at most one secondary need; filters match either. AI capabilities and vehicle-use reasons may overlap. Embodiment and autonomy are separate; autonomy is described in scenario details. Filtering preserves original TOP numbers.
Study core benefits in the first 20; expand validation for 21–50; retain 51–100 as exploration candidates. Investment order also depends on project capability and evidence. Ranking a future autonomous-movement concept does not establish feasibility.
Coverage is not exhaustive discovery
The 8-by-9 map counts associations across 100 candidates; one scenario can occupy multiple cells. Filled cells do not mean all needs have been found, and empty cells do not prove no need exists. This version checks coverage of current concepts without claiming exhaustiveness.
Validate outcomes before deepening the product
Each entry records its concept, expected benefit, alternative, validation question and research horizon. Compare outcomes, total effort, correction and confirmation costs, and voluntary repeat choice.
Near-term trial, conditional research and future exploration are working hypotheses, not performance, safety or production certifications. Study games, cinema and complex spatial experiences in safely parked conditions first. Vehicle movement depends on dedicated systems and site conditions.
